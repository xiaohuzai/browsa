Stay on the page. Ask beside it.
browsa is a Chrome / Edge extension that brings the page you're reading to the AI you already use.

Follow four connected workflows:
• Read articles, blogs and technical docs: attach a Transformer article, ask a question, then select part of the answer to ask a follow-up. View LaTeX equations and Mermaid / Graphviz diagrams right in the side panel.
• Watch YouTube / Bilibili videos: turn interview highlights into a timeline and click a timestamp to check the original moment.
• Switch Agents: choose to carry the current conversation into another Agent and keep talking.
• Continue in your Agent: copy messages, download images, save and restore conversations, then resume the same Agent session—with Codex as the example.

The extension is free and open source (MIT). Connect your own model API, Ollama, or a supported Agent. Model/API costs depend on your provider.
